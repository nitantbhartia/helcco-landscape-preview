How a submittal moves

  1. Walk the site with the client and listen to their vision and ideas.
  2. Work with budget and begin an initial design.
  3. Build previously acquired design plans, or create a design custom to you.
  4. Compliant to all City Regulations and HOA Guidelines. Licensed and insured.
  5. Once hardscape and landscape drawings are approved by the client, begin the permit process.
  6. Once all approvals are received from the City/HOA, begin construction.
