Our designs and construction

We understand every space is unique and each project is different, therefore we begin our process with our clients:

  1. We walk the site with our client and listen to their vision and ideas.
  2. Work with budget and begin an initial design.
  3. HELCCO can build your previously acquired design plans or help to create a design custom to you.
  4. Compliant to all City Regulations and HOA Guidelines. Licensed and Insured.
  5. Once Hardscape and Landscape Drawings are approved by our client we begin the permit process.
  6. Once all approvals are received from the City/HOA, we begin construction... Yayy!!!
